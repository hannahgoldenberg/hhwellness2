Step by Step

How HairHelper Works

Step 01

Slip On

Place HairHelper over your head like a headband — designed to keep overhead reaching to a minimum.

Step 02

Slide to Crown

Glide it up to the crown of your head, gathering hair as it moves.

Step 03

Press & Pull

Press the clasp down and pull both cords simultaneously to tighten and secure.

Done ✓

Styled ✓

Hair secured. To release: press the clasp and pull gently to loosen. All done independently.

🔓

To release: Press down on the clasp mechanism and pull gently — the cords loosen and HairHelper slides off. The full process is designed to minimize overhead arm movement and hand dexterity. Need more detail? Read the full instructional guide →

Designed For

Who We Designed HairHelper For

🧊

Frozen Shoulder

Adhesive capsulitis severely limits overhead range of motion. HairHelper is designed to reduce the need to raise your arms above shoulder height.

🧠

Stroke Recovery

For women with post-stroke hemiplegia or reduced upper limb function, HairHelper is designed to be as one-hand-friendly as possible.

🩹

Shoulder Surgery Recovery

Rotator cuff repair, shoulder replacement, and other procedures leave months of restricted motion. Always follow your care team's movement restrictions before using HairHelper.

♿

Spinal Cord Injury

For women with cervical or thoracic SCI affecting upper extremity function, HairHelper is designed to require minimal arm elevation and dexterity; suitability varies by individual.

💪

Shoulder Impingement

Chronic pain shouldn't mean dependence on others for something as personal as styling your own hair.

✨

Anyone Wanting Ease

HairHelper's one-step mechanism is designed to be faster and gentler than traditional ties, with less creasing and tugging.

What makes HairHelper different from a regular hair tie?
Unlike traditional hair ties or scrunchies, HairHelper is designed to minimize overhead arm movement and avoid repeated looping. It slides on like a headband and tightens with a single press-and-pull motion at the crown — designed with women who have difficulty raising their arms above their head in mind.
Can HairHelper be used with one hand?
HairHelper is designed to be as one-hand-friendly as possible. The slip-on headband application requires minimal bilateral coordination, and the clasp-and-cord tightening mechanism is designed to be operable with limited dexterity, refined with occupational therapist input throughout design.
