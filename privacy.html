Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

H&H Wellness, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website and online store for H&H Wellness, Inc., located at 17 Old Kings Hwy, Old Greenwich, CT, 06870. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and purchase our products.

By using our website and purchasing our products, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect information about you in a range of ways when you use our website or place an order:

  • Personal Data: While buying our products, you voluntarily provide us with personally identifiable information, such as your name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and phone number.
  • Payment Data: All payments are processed securely through our third-party payment processor, Stripe. We do not store, view, or collect your credit card numbers or financial credentials. Stripe's use of your personal information is governed by their respective privacy policy.
  • Communications: If you contact us, we collect the information you choose to include in your message.
  • Log and Device Data: Our website servers and hosting providers may automatically collect information when you access our site, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and the pages you view.
  • Security Data (Google reCAPTCHA): Our checkout button is protected by Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to prevent fraud and bot activity. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and browser data, and sends it to Google for analysis. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Embedded Content: Our product page embeds video from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, and our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address and browser information when the content loads.

2. HEALTH INFORMATION

We do not ask for, and you do not need to provide, any health information to browse our site or buy our products. We do not use your purchase or browsing activity to infer any health condition, and we do not sell or share such information for advertising. If you choose to tell us about a health condition (for example, in feedback), we use it only to respond to you and to improve our products, and we do not sell or share it. Please avoid sending us sensitive health details.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect to run our business efficiently, including to:

  • Process, fulfill, and ship your orders.
  • Send you order confirmations, tracking numbers, shipping-delay notices, and other updates about your order.
  • Respond to customer service inquiries and support needs.
  • Send you product news or marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing email.
  • Comply with legal and financial obligations (such as tax reporting).
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and monitor against theft.

4. SHARING YOUR INFORMATION

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us run our business:

  • Payment Processors: We share your transactional data with Stripe to process credit card payments.
  • Shipping Carriers: We share your name and shipping address with our delivery partners (such as USPS, UPS, or FedEx) to deliver your packages.
  • Website and Security Providers: Our hosting, security, and verification providers (such as GitHub, Cloudflare, and Google reCAPTCHA) process technical data to operate and protect our site.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities, or to protect our rights, property, or safety or that of others.
  • Business Transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Privacy Policy.

5. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

We and our service providers (such as Google reCAPTCHA and YouTube) may use cookies and similar technologies on our website to keep the site secure, prevent fraud, and improve your shopping experience. Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default, but you can remove or reject cookies in your browser settings if you prefer; some features, such as checkout verification, may not work without them. Our site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals.

6. SECURITY OF YOUR DATA

We use commercial, industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal information. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

7. DATA RETENTION

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, such as fulfilling orders, providing customer support, and meeting legal, tax, and accounting requirements, and then delete or de-identify it.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your location (including if you live in California or another state with a consumer privacy law), you may have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request that your personal data be corrected, request that your personal data be permanently deleted from our records, or opt out of certain uses of your data. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. To make a request, please contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before completing your request.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website and products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable law requires). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. INTERNATIONAL USERS

We are based in the United States, and your information will be processed and stored in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

11. THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Our website contains links to and embedded content from third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.
